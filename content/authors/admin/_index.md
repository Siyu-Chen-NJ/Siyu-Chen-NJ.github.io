I am a Ph.D. candidate in Mechanical Engineering at **The University of Texas at Austin**, working in the [System Integration & Design Informatics Laboratory (SiDi Lab)](https://sidilab.net/). I expect to complete my Ph.D. in **May 2027**.

My research develops optimization and learning methods for **reliable engineering design decisions under uncertainty**. I study how multiple agents should explore design alternatives, exchange information, and decide when to stop when evaluations are expensive, operating conditions vary, and decisions depend on other decision-makers. My doctoral work spans cost-aware, distributed, robust, and competitive Bayesian optimization.

My longer-term research agenda is to develop **generalizable agentic AI for engineering design**: AI partners that learn reusable optimization strategies from experience and collaborate with engineers across coupled systems, with decisions grounded in physical constraints, uncertainty, and engineering evidence.

Before UT Austin, I worked on Gaussian process-based consensus control for uncertain multi-agent systems at the Technical University of Munich. In summer 2026, I was an LLM & Agentic AI R&D intern at Robert Bosch LLC, studying Bayesian optimization with unknown constraints.
